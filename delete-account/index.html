Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go. If you'd like to delete your Timeboxer account, please follow the instructions below.

⚠️ Warning: Deleting your account will permanently remove all your data including tasks, schedules, and account information. This action cannot be undone.

ℹ️ If you have an active subscription, please cancel it through the App Store or Google Play before deleting your account to avoid future charges.

How to delete your account:

  1. Open the Timeboxer app on your device
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete my account"
  4. Enter your email address to confirm
  5. Tap "Delete My Account"
